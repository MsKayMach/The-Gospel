1The Truth – Christ Jesus

Blessed be the Name of our Lord and Saviour, King Jesus Christ. Greetings to the reader of this piece which outlines the steps of salvation through Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour and Redeemer, by God’s grace, His love and great sacrifice. May the Holy Spirit reveal God’s love for you, may The Spirit of God give you wisdom to discern and accept The Truth- Lord Jesus Christ, the Only Way to save your eternal soul. This piece will mainly focus on salvation, which is the gift of God and the spiritual armour of God.

As Christians we believe that the Bible is the Word of God, inspired by God and one of the ways in which God communicates to us. 2 Tim 3:16 (KJV) “All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness”. We read the Bible with conviction that it is God Almighty’s Word and we acknowledge that He is communicating to us. Psalm 119: 105 (KJV) “ Thy Word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path.” This booklet is therefore based on the Bible and the believer’s faith in Lord Jesus Christ as the Author of our Christian faith.

2Creation and Provision of Man

Gen 1:27 (KJV) “so God created man in His own image, in the Image of God created He him, male and female created He them”. Man originates from God, His love for man made Him create man in His resemblance, to be like Him. Man is the only creation that the Creator created in His own Image. According to Gen 1:26 (NIV) “ God said let us make man in our own image.” We have no doubt that we were created by God, in His likeness. Gen 2:7 (KJV) “And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and man became a living soul” We are God’s outstanding creation with Gods breath, the living breath of the Living God, in His Image, for His pleasure. A relationship between God and His creation(man) -can be seen throughout the Scripture.

In the book ofGenesis 1:28 (KJV) we learn that God gave man authority and dominion over every living thing upon the earth. Genesis 1:29-30 (KJV) And God said, “Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of the earth, and every tree which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed, to you it shall be for meat. And to every beast of the earth, and to every foul of the air, and to everything that creepeth upon the earth, where there is life, I have given every green herb for meat: and it was so”. God Almighty made man in His own image, blessed him, provided food to eat and gave him authority over the earth.

3The Disobedience and the Fall of Man

The first book of Moses records that God had a loving relationship with man, The Lord God put man in the garden of Eden to freely eat out of every tree of the garden. “But out of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shall not eat thereof, for in the day that thou eateth thereof, thou shall surely die.”Gen 3:1c-4(KJV) “Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?” the serpent, the deceiver asked Eve. The woman said unto the serpent, “we may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden, but of the fruit of the tree, which is in the midst of the garden, God hath said, Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye touch it, lest ye die. And the serpent said to the woman, ye shall not surely die.”

The serpent deceived the woman, she ate of the forbidden tree, and she also gave her husband, Adam to eat. The enemy knew that after eating, they were going to die spiritually- the evil serpent deliberately deceived the woman to disobey God out of his evil-nature. The Bible teaches that the wages of sin is death, therefore the disobedience of man led to death. In the book of Romans 5:12we learn that just as sin entered the world through one man, Adam, and death through sin, man is born a sinner with a sinful nature. In Romans 3:23 Apostle Paul says all have sinned and come short of The Glory of God. 1 John 1:10 (KJV) “If we say we have not sinned, we make Him a liar, and His Word is not in us.

4Confession and Repentance

Confession is to acknowledge sinfulness or wrongdoing. 1 John 1:9 (KJV) states that if we confess our sins to God, He forgives us and cleanse us from all unrighteousness. In the book of Daniel 9:20, we read that Daniel prayed and confessed his sins to the Lord God. In*Prov. 28:13*,The Bible warns that he who conceals his sins does not prosper, but the one who confesses and renounces them finds mercy. We must confess our sins, repent, and turn to God.

Repentanceis the forsaking of sins. Acts 2:38 (NIV) Peter replied, “Repent and be baptised, each one of you in the name of The LORD Jesus Christ for the remission of your sins and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.” The Lord Jesus came to this world to set us free and reconcile us back to God Almighty. If we do not repent from our sins, we shall perish.

We must acknowledge that we are sinners, confess our sins and repent. Change the way we live and live a righteous life, change the way we do things and do the will of God. Stop worshipping idols and worship God Almighty, our Creator. Change the way we think and have the mind of Christ, by the leading of the Holy Spirit. Prov. 23:7 says that what a man thinks is what a man really is. The way we think determines who we are. If you want to change the way you live, then change your mind by feeding it with The Truth of God’s Word (Christian Life Bible School -Foundation Doctrine Vol. 1 P 14).

There are only two options which will determine the future eternity of an individual, repent or perish. John 3:16 (KJV) For God so loved the world that He gave His Only begotten Son so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life. John 10:27-28(KJV), My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: And I give unto them eternal life, and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand”. It is clear that the word perish is the opposite of eternal life. The LORD Jesus Christ Himself, says His sheep shall not perish, they shall have eternal life.

This shows that those who are not His, who do not believe in Him, who have not accepted salvation through believing in Him as their Lord and Saviour-will NOTinherit eternal life, but eternal death, they will perish. In John 5:24 the Lord Jesus says he who hears my voice and believe Him who sent me has eternal life and shall not come into judgement and has passed from death to life. John 1:12 (KJV) “But as many as received Him, to them gave He power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name”. When we receive Him and believe in LORD Jesus Christ, we pass from death and become children of God. What a blessing to be one of God’s family members, to be a child of the Most High.

According to Scriptures in the first book of the Bible, the body is made of the dust from the ground and man is the living soul inside the body. The Bible states that while the dust will return to dust, the soul lives for eternity either- eternal life or eternal death. We therefore must make a conscious decision while we are still on this body, and choose eternal life though faith in LORD Jesus Christ, believe in Him and accept Him as our Lord and Saviour.

5Salvation Through Christ Jesus

There is no other way to eternal life, other than salvation through LORD Jesus Christ. Salvation is an act of faith; believing in the Lord, having faith in Him, receiving Him and accepting His Lordship and confessing with our mouths His Lordship in our lives. Romans 10:9 (KJV) “That if thou shall confess with thy mouth The Lord Jesus and shall believe in thine heart that God hath raised Him from the dead, thou shall be saved.” Salvation is only through Christ Jesus -Eph 2:8 clearly states that salvation is not of ourselves, it is by God’s grace that we are saved, a gift from God. In Rev 3:20 the LORD Jesus Christ says He stands at the door(heart) and knocks, if any man hears His voice and opens, He will come in and eat with him.

It is God’s desire that all man be saved and escape eternal death. God sent His Only Son because of His love for us; our Creator loves us.In 1 Tim 2:4 God wants all men to be saved and come the knowledge Truth, LORD Jesus Christ.

A person is born again by accepting the LORD Jesus Christ- that is re-birth of the spirit. In*John 3:3 our LORD Jesus Christ said to Nicodemus, “Verily, Verily, I say unto thee, except a man be born again, he CANNOT see the Kingdom of God” The Lord Jesus further explains that except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he CANNOT enter the Kingdom of God(*v.5). That which is born of the flesh is flesh: and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.

Mark16:16(KJV) “He who believes and is baptized will be saved.” After a conscious decision to receive Him, follow and be His follower. Col 2:12“Buried with Him in baptism wherein also ye are risen with Him through faith in the working of God who raised Him from the dead”. Baptism signifies that we are risen with our LORD Jesus Christ, it is full immersion in water and be baptized in the name of the Father, of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

Acts 8:12 “When they believed Phillip preaching the things concerning the Kingdom of God and the Name of Jesus, they were baptized both men and women.” Baptism is not sprinkling, it is full immersion of the whole body, just like a dead body is buried, when the person comes out of the water, that signifies that the person is risen with Christ. We dedicate children to God; we don’t baptize them. When they are old enough to make their own decision to accept and follow Christ, they are then baptized.

The Bibles teaches that those who believe and receive Him are called children of God. The Word of God clearly states that those who receive Him, He(The Lord) gives them power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on His Name. His children are new creatures, according to 2 Cor 5:17, old things have passed away, they have a new covenant with God, a new relationship with our Creator.

Baptism is an act of faith, believing that, getting into the water; the body fully covered by water, you are buried just like Christ was buried. Coming out of the water, is resurrection just like our Lord who was risen from the dead. By faith, the old man is buried, and the new man re-born, partaking in the burial and resurrection of our Saviour.

6Prayer of Faith

It is crucial to be attentive to what God says to you through His Word, also communicate to Him through prayer. A child of God knows that God loves him/her, and He cares -He is always with them(His children) and for them.In the book of 1Thes 5:17, Apostle Paul says we must pray without ceasing, know that it is God’s desire to answer your prayers as your Heavenly Father. Mark 11:24 “Whatever things you ask when you pray, believe that you receive them, and you will have them”

7Trust God in All Areas of Your Life

Prov. 3:5 (KJV)“Trust in the Lord**with all your **heart, lean not unto own understanding”. Let the Lord be the centre of your life. Hold on to Him and abide in Him knowing that as a born-again child of the Most High God, you are safe in the hands of our Heavenly Father, The Creator of Heaven and earth, The Omnipotent and Almighty.

It is important to note that as a born again( born of the Spirit), know that there is a spiritual battle in the spiritual realm. It is a spiritual battle between the Kingdom of Light and the kingdom of darkness. Thanks to God Almighty who is on our side, with Him on our side we are safe in the palm of His Hands. As a soldier in the spiritual battle- it is imperative to put on the full Armour of God as instructed in Ephesians 6 :10-18.

8The Armour of God

As Christian we put on the spiritual armour knowing that greater is He who is on our side, our Shield. The spiritual battle is real, it requires spiritual protective clothing(armour) and be protected against the enemy- the devil, and his demons. In 1John 4:4 we are encouraged and blessed to note that greater is He who is in us that the devil who is in the world.

In 2 Cor 10:4 the Word of God says for the weapon of our warfare are not carnal but mighty in God for pulling down the strongholds. We need awareness and knowledge of the full armour of God that we must put on.

The Helmet of Salvation

The helmet protects the head, because an injury on the head can affect the body as well. The Bible instructs us to put on salvation as a helmet. Apostle Paul writes to the Ephesians*in chapter 6 v.17a(KJV).*saying “Take the helmet of salvation,” We take it by faith and put it on- protecting the head. As a new creature, saved by grace, know that you are saved, salvation through Christ Jesus. That salvation is the spiritual helmet that protects us from the evil one, the devil. The devil knows he wont touch us because we are saved by the precious blood of The Lamb of God. He knows that we are marked as the children of God, we have the seal(mark) of the Holy Spirit. Ephesians 1:13 (KJV) “In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the Word of God, the Gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that Holy Spirit of promise.”

As a soldier in the spiritual battle, salvation(helmet) protects the head and its parts, it protects the way we think, what we see, what we hear, and what we speak. Our actions and behaviour are the result of what we think. As the helmet protects the brain, salvation through Christ protects the mind. In Phil 2:5 Apostle Paul says “let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ.” We must have the mind of Christ through the help of the Holy Spirit in us. The Holy Spirit guides us, guides our mind which controls our actions and behaviour. The Holy Spirit teaches us things of the spirit and reveals the will of God in our lives.

With this helmet of salvation, we are protected from the enemy who attacks the way people think, knowing that all actions begin in the mind. We are safe because of the helmet of salvation; our actions, what we do, what we speak and how we behave is the leading of The Holy Spirit in us. This is only possible when the helmet of salvation is in place. The LORD Jesus Christ has promised that He will put His Words in our minds, and the born of the Spirit will love the Lord with their minds. What a blessed assurance from our Saviour! Glory to the Most High God. We know who we are, whose we are, who is in us and with us.

The Breastplate of Righteousness

When we read the Bible in 2 Thess. 5:8 the Bible says we must put on faith and love as breastplate. The breastplate is a protective armour that protects the heart, which is an important organ in the body. Prov. 4:28 King Solomon says, “above all else guard your heart for it is the wellspring of life.” It is imperative to protect the spiritual heart for it is vital for our spiritual wellbeing. As cautious and responsible soldiers in the spiritual battle we must put on the breastplate of righteousness to protect our spiritual bodies from spiritual harm by the enemy, Satan.

Righteousness (faith and love) is the gift that is imputed to the born again-it comes through faith in the LORD Jesus Christ our Lord and our Saviour. Rom.* *4:9 says, ‘faith was credited to Abraham for righteousness’. In Rom. 3:22 we learn that righteousness is through faith in our LORD Jesus Christ. The Bible warns believers not to be unequally yoked together with unbelievers, for there is no communion between righteousness and unrighteousness, between the light and darkness

The Belt of Truth

Our LORD Jesus Christ says in John 14:6 “I am the Way, the Truth, and the Light’. Ye shall know the Truth (the LORD Jesus Christ), and the Truth shall set you free. The King of kings and The Lord of lords came to this world to set us free from the bondage of Satan. Our Saviour has set us free, we stand firm in our faith knowing we have our Saviour-we have the belt in place as an armour.

Stand as a Child of God “having your loins girt about with truth” Eph. 6:14b(KJV).As born again, new creatures, it is important to note that according to Eph.6:12 we are born again into a battlefield against principalities, against powers, and rulers of the darkness of this world. The battle is not physical/ carnal but spiritual, it is not against people- but against Satan and his demons. The enemy uses demons and his agents of darkness to fight, destroy, if possible, to kill believers. Ignorant believers without the full armour of God in place, are vulnerable to enemy’s attack. Thanks to God Almighty who is on our side of the battle, the enemy has no powers – the One on our side is for us and with us all the time. The Bible says with Him on our side who can be against us. God is Mighty, Terrible, and Great in battle. With the Belt of Truth, we are more than conquerors. Rom 8:37 (KJV) “in all things ye are more than conquerors though Him that loved us”. We must stand strong, firm, rooted in Christ and girt about with Christ Jesus, the Truth, by the power of the Holy Spirit knowing that greater is He in us than the devil who is in the world. Soldiers keep their swords in their belts; together with the belt of Truth is the sword.

The Sword of the Spirit

Without the sword the soldier is not equipped. The Word of God is the sword of the spirit, alive, active, and sharper than any double-edged sword- as stated in the Book of Heb.4:12. Lord Jesus is the Word that became flesh. When we read the Gospel according to John 1:1(KJV) we read that in the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God and the Word was God and the Word, Lord Jesus, became flesh and dwelt among us(v.14).The Bible says in 1John.5:7*, “There are Three that bear witness in Heaven, The Father, The Word , and The Holy Spirit, and the Three are One”. All that is created was created through Him (The Word). *Ephe.6:12 instructs believers to take the sword of the spirit, which is the Word of God.

Lord Jesus is The Living Word of God, the Divine Armour of God. Having the Lord as our Sword, we are more than conquerors and victory is ours. Believers also equip themselves with the written Word of God from The Holy Bible, the powerful sword to use against the enemy.

The Divine Sword (Jesus) is powerful and there is power in His Name. Power to heal, to cast out demons, to raise the dead, to perform miracles and we can do all things through Him. The disciples had toiled the whole night but did not catch fish. When the Lord Jesus told them to let down the net, knowing the power in His Word, they obeyed and caught a great multitude of fish. David killed the giant Goliath in the Name of the Lord, a powerful Sword. As believers we must read the Bible, meditate on the Word, and live according to the Word( Sword). We can do all things through Christ(Phil 4:13)-It is the Name of our Redeemer, our Saviour, The Mighty One who conquered the enemy.

We face the enemy trusting the One who defeated the enemy, the One given the Name that is above all names and seated at the right hand of God The Father. Phil. 2:9 “wherefore God also hath highly exalted Him and given Him the Name which is above all names”. We face the enemy trusting the Sword (Jesus)that is with us always. In Matthew 28:20 the Lord says “I am with you always, even unto the end of the world.” We conquer in the Name of the One who has Power; with Him nothing is impossible. This is only possible when we know, believe and have faith in the One who gave us power, as the believer you must know who you are- the child of The Most High God. The Bible says faith comes by hearing the Word of God. Together with the Sword, we must have the shield.

The Shield of Faith

Eph.6:16“Above all, taking the shield of faith with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked one.” Faith is the foundation of our spiritual life. From the onset when we are born again, we are born by faith, we continue in our spiritual walk by faith, with our LORD Jesus Christ as the Author and the Finisher of our faith. Faith is the destructive weapon in the spiritual battle that destroys and disarms Satan, his demons, and his agents.

Heb.11:1 “Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” In Heb.11:6Apostle Paul says without faith it is impossible to please God. When we act in faith, God Almighty steps in and take over whatever spiritual battle we are facing. The Scripture says He is The Mighty warrior and great in battle.

Knowing that greater is He who is in us than the devil who is in the world. Knowing that the Creator of heaven and earth, is our Shield and Buckler, our Fortress, our Rock, our Strength, and our Shield-we face the enemy with confidence and faith in the One who doesn’t lose, the One who fights our battles. This is only possible when we do not doubt our Heavenly Father, when we have faith in Him “our Present Help in times of trouble.” Psalm 46:1

It is crucial to understand that the spiritual battle is real, it is the battle between the Kingdom of light and the kingdom of darkness, the Kingdom of God, and the kingdom of Satan. Praise and Honour to God for His love for us. God has assigned His angels to protect us from the enemy. We are blessed for the gift of salvation through Christ, the devil doesn’t have power over us. God Almighty is our Shepherd, in Psalm 23:4(KJV) king David says “ though I walk though the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for Thou art with me.” If we trust God and we have faith in Him, we fear no evil trusting the One who can do all things, Powerful and Mighty. In chapter 6, Apostle Paul writes to the Ephesians telling them to put on the armour of God and stand praying always with all supplications in Spirit. Having done all- to stand.

9Having Done All – Stand

The born-again child of God must stand firmly rooted in Christ. The devil may try different tricks to disturb, to deceive, to destroy and to kill. It is very important to be alert and watchful, properly covered with the full armour of God, having done all -to stand as soldiers in the army of God. John 15:4-5 (KJV)* *“Abide in Me and Me in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abides in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in Me. I am the vine, ye are the branches; he that abides in Me and I in him, the same bringeth much fruit; for without Me ye can do nothing”. We must abide in our Lord by faith, make Christ the Lord of our lives, depend on Him, put our trust in Him, and submit to His Lordship. Our purpose for living is for His glory, may the Lord be glorified and highly exalted.

Gal.3: 26-27(KJV)* “For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus. For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ”. The same way we put on the armour of God by faith, we put on Christ by faith. When we put on Christ, we put on Life, eternal life. Eph.3:17*“ That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love”. We stand firmly in Him knowing that Christ is with us and has promised to never leave us. We know that the Lord is faithful, we stand on His promises.

Reading, meditating, and believing the written word of God increases the level of our faith and confidence in our Christ Jesus( the Living Word). Our faith in Christ helps us to stand firmly, trusting Him, having assurance that Christ is our Rock, The Divine Foundation of our faith. We stand rooted, with perseverance even during testing, trials, and tribulations. In John 16:33(KJV) the Lord says, “In this world ye shall have many tribulations, be of good cheer, I have overcome the world.” Our Saviour has overcome the enemy; we stand with our hope in the One who is Mighty and Faithful. Psalm 121:7 “The lord shall preserve thee from all evil: He shall preserve thy soul”

1 Peter 4: 12-13(KJV) “Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trials which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened to you. But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s suffering that, when His glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.” James 1:2-3(KJV) My brethren, count it all joy when ye shall fall into divers temptations knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience”

We stand in trials, temptations, persecutions and in tribulation, with the hope in the One who in with us, in us and for us. The Bible says greater is He who is in us that the devil who is in the world.1 Cor. 10:13 says God doesn’t allow us to be tempted beyond our ability, even in the temptation God will provide the way to escape. Glory to God who is watching over us always. We keep standing knowing that great is the reward of the believer who will persevere up to the end, as stated in Rom 8:18“For I reckon that the suffering of this present time are not worth to be compared with the glory which will be revealed in us.”

The Word of God encourages us to hold on fast the profession of our faith without wavering. The Word of God says, blessed are they that are persecuted for their righteousness, because they will inherit the kingdom of God. We stand because we know we shall spend eternity with our Lord and Saviour- Lord Jesus Christ who bought us with His precious blood. We know that through Him we will spend eternity in Heaven. The Bible says those who believe in Christ shall not perish but have eternal life. Those who do not believe, shall not have eternal life- but eternal death, for not believing the Only begotten Son of God.

Luke 16: 23-24 “And in hell he lifted up his eyes, being in torment …for I am tormented in this flame.” Some people argue that God will not send people in hell, a place of torment. John 3:16 says for God so loved the world, that he gave His only begotten Son, so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life. It is stated in the scripture that whoever does not believe the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of God abides in him.

God wants all men to be saved, God does not want anyone to perish hence He made a way for reconciliation and salvation through Christ Jesus.Tim 2:4 says “God not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance” John 3:18 (KJV) …he that believeth not is condemned already because he hath not believed in the name of the Only begotten Son of God.

There are only two places of eternity for the souls. Eternal life in heaven or eternal death in hell. The unrighteous souls, not covered by the blood of the Lamb of God, not born again, whose sins are not forgiven, who have not accepted the LORD Jesus Christ, whose names are not written in the Book of Life; shall spend eternity in hell fire. God is Holy and Righteous , Omnipresent, and The Most High, He commands us to be holy for He is Holy.

The non – believers are not covered by the Blood of Christ, while the believers are covered by the precious blood of the Lamb of God. The Bible teaches that he that has the Son has eternal life, he that does not have the Son does not have eternal life.

Our God is Love and God of love, He is also The God of judgement. 2 Peter 2:4-5(KJV)**“For** if God spared not the angels that sinned…And spared not the world…And turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes…The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptation, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgement to be punished.” There are two options, eternal life through Christ who is LIFE or eternal death in hell fire for not accepting LIFE - the LORD Jesus Christ.

Make the right choice and choose The LORD Jesus Christ today as your Lord and Saviour. Pray to God to forgive you your sins and cleanse you from all unrighteousness. Believe in the LORD Jesus Christ, the Only WAY to Heaven.

Jesus said unto him, “I AM THE WAY, THE TRUTH, AND THE LIFE: no one cometh unto the Father but by Me” John 14:6 (KJV) I AM THE DOOR, by me if any man enters in, he shall be saved…(John 10:9)

The One(Lord Jesus) who is One with God, loves us and prayed for us. In John 17(KJV) we learn that Jesus Christ prayed that God would keep us and sanctify us with His Word. The Lord’s desire is for us to be with Him where He is and behold His glory. Our Lord Jesus Christ is still praying for us, as recorded in Rom 8:34(KJV) that Lord Jesus Christ is seated at the right hand of The Father praying for us. Glory and honour to our Heavenly Father. InJohn 15:9(KJV) our Lord says “as The Father has loved me, so have I loved you.”

God Loves Us

God’s love for us is beyond our imagination, this cannot be emphasized enough, God loved us so much that He sent His Only begotten Son to reconcile us to Himself. It is recorded in John 3:16(KJV) that for God so loved the world that He gave His Only begotten Son, so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life. In John 17:23(KJV)Lord Jesus says “I in them, and Thou in Me, that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know that Thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as You loved me”. What a blessed assurance, we thank God The Father for His love for us.

Apostle Paul penned to the Ephesians, in chapter 2:4-5(KJV) “But God, who is rich in mercy, for the great love wherewith He loved us, even when we were dead in sins, hath quickened us together with Christ( by grace ye are saved).It is written “I am crucified with Christ nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me; and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God,who loved me, and gave Himself for me (Gal 2:20 KJV).” As believers in Lord Jesus Christ, we know that Christ lives in us, and we live in Him.

Eternal life through Lord Jesus Christ

In John 17:1-2(KJV) The Bible records that our Lord lifted up His eyes to Heaven and prayed “Father the hour is come, glorify Thy Son, that Thy Son also may glorify Thee; as thou has given Him power over all flesh, that he should give eternal life to as many as Thou hast given Him.”

It is God’s desire that all men come to the knowledge of truth, and be saved(1Tim 2:4KJV). Our Lord Jesus Christ has power to give eternal life to all who believe in Him. In**the book ofJohn 10:27-28(KJV)** our Lord Jesus Christ says “my sheep hear my voice and I know them, and they follow me, and I give unto them eternal life and they shall never perish, neither shall any men pluck them out of my hand.”

“Verily, verily, I say unto you, he that heareth my word and believeth in Him that sent me, has everlasting life, he shall not come into condemnation but is passed from death to eternal life.”(John 5:24KJV). Eternal life is only through Lord Jesus Christ who came to this world to save us and reconcile us to God, it is written in 2Cor 5:18(KJV) that all things are of God who reconciled us to Himself through Jesus Christ.

God’s desire to be with us forever

The Holy Bible inIsaiah 40:28(KJV) says that The Lord is The Everlasting God, The Creator of the ends of the earth. InPsalm 145:13(KJV), we read that God’s Kingdom is an everlasting Kingdom and His dominion endureth through all generations. It is our Everlasting Father’s desire that we spend everlasting life with Him in His everlasting Kingdom, through faith in His only begotten Son, who has power to give us everlasting life.

God shall preserve us from evil

Our God is Faithful, and Trustworthy, we trust in God who always keeps His promises. God has promised to preserve us from all evil,a song of degrees, in Psalm 121:7(KJV) says “The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil, He shall preserve thy soul.” As believers we are in a spiritual battle, the enemy(satan) hates believers in Christ, he wants to win as many souls as possible for his kingdom of darkness and lead souls to eternal death(hell).

We thank God Almighty who protects us from all evil and from all satanic attacks. In 2Thess 3:3(KJV) the Word of God says “ The Lord is Faithful, who shall stablish you and keep you from evil” The battle is real and it is not against flesh and blood, not against people, but against the devil and his angels. Glory and honour to God Almighty, our Faithful God always delivers us from all evil, it is also recorded in 2Tim 4:18 that The Lord will always deliver me from every evil work,and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom,to whom be glory for ever and ever. Psalm 91:11 says He shall give His angels charge over thee and keep thee in all thy ways. God’s angels are always with us all the time protecting us.God’s mighty hand upon us is amazing, He knew us and loved us before we knew Him.
